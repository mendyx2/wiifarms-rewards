The season pays for itself at about 63% sell-through

Prize commitments are fixed when a season is approved, so the result depends on how many cards sell. Move the slider to see the campaign position the operator dashboard shows, using the requirements' own illustrative 20% for operating costs.

At 40% (the requirements' worked example) the balance is −₦241.74m; break-even is where sales cover commitments plus costs. Instant cash is released in levels as cards sell, which limits early exposure. Affiliate fees (10% of referred sales) and payment fees come on top.

How a player takes part

1

Buy

₦500 a card, up to 10 a day, by bank transfer, card or wallet. Age, identity and spending limits are checked first.

2

Scratch

Swipe the silver foil. The result was sealed before sales opened, and the phone checks it against the published fingerprint.

3

Mega Draw

Every card, win or lose, is entered. The draw is public, overseen by an independent auditor, and anyone can re-run it.

4

Collect

Cash goes to the player's wallet; goods are collected at approved stockists with a one-time code and photo ID.

Controls a regulator and an auditor can check

Results sealed before sale. Every card's outcome is fixed and fingerprinted; nobody can change who wins.
Independent draw report. The draw is re-checked from the records, fingerprinted and attested by the appointed auditor.
Two people for anything that matters. Approvals, payouts, fee changes and access changes each need a second person.
Exact accounting. A double-entry ledger in kobo, daily reconciliation, and twelve management reports.
Responsible play. 18+ only, verified identity, spending limits, breaks and self-exclusion.
Security. Two-step sign-in for staff, encrypted secrets and a tamper-evident audit trail.

Built and tested

Player app (English and Hausa), operations app, affiliate portal, stockist portal, public website, Mega Draw with its report, and management reports. Checked by about 85 automated tests.

Sales engine

Authorised digital affiliates earn 10% of the sales they bring. Approved marketing only; no downlines; complaints investigated.

Next steps

State licence, engine certification, live payment provider and bank collections, then the inaugural 90-day season.
